Our programme

Sewing Machine Repair Programme

We give unused sewing machines a second life. Donated machines are serviced and rehomed with people and community groups who will use them, keeping them out of landfill and putting a valuable skill within reach.

a second life!
How it works

From cupboard to community

  1. 1

    Donate

    Give an unused sewing machine a second life.

    Instead of sitting in a cupboard or heading to landfill, your machine goes back to work.

  2. 2

    Service & repair

    Machines are cleaned, checked and repaired.

    Each one is logged with its details and photos, so we know its history.

  3. 3

    Rehome

    Ready machines go to people & community groups.

    Matched in order from our waiting list and collected from West Auckland.

Who it helps

Anyone can apply

For yourself, or on behalf of a community group or organisation.

  • Machines are matched in the order people applied
  • Priority for people in or near West Auckland
  • For mending, learning or making for your whānau

Have a machine you no longer use?

Donate it, and we'll make sure it gets back into a maker's hands.
